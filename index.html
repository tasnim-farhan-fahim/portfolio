Computer Graphics

Urban Train Station

C OpenGL GLUT 2.0
OpenGL GLUT Viewport • 30 FPS Cycle

A computer graphics project that presents an illustrated urban train station environment, demonstrating core computer graphics concepts using pure C , OpenGL , and GLUT 2.0 .

The scene features geometric modeling, custom lighting vectors, smooth color-space interpolation, and dynamic temporal transitions programmed without high-level 3D engines.

Six Time-Based Visual Environments

A continuously looping scene that transitions seamlessly across 6 distinct diurnal atmospheric atmospheres:

Morning Dawn Bright Midday Golden Afternoon Crimson Sunset Twilight Dusk Deep Night
